Highlights

- Interval coverage is a design-based population parameter, like map accuracy
- Its Horvitz-Thompson estimator is exactly, not approximately, design-unbiased
- CV+ intervals are mildly conservative and 16% sharper than quantile RF
- Clustered calibration drops coverage to 0.86 against a nominal 0.90
- Spatial-block calibration restores nominal coverage, reaching 0.95

Highlights

- Interval coverage is a design-based population parameter, like map accuracy
- Its Horvitz-Thompson estimator is exactly, not approximately, design-unbiased
- Spatial-block calibration holds 0.93-0.95 across every size and geometry
- Clustered calibration drops coverage to 0.81-0.88, by design geometry
- More data in the same clusters lowers coverage further; more clusters raises it

Highlights

- Interval coverage is a design-based population parameter, just as map accuracy is
- Its Horvitz-Thompson estimator is exactly, not approximately, design-unbiased
- CV+ intervals are calibrated and 17% sharper than quantile regression forests
- Clustered calibration collapses coverage to 0.84 against a nominal 0.90
- Spatial-block calibration restores nominal coverage, reaching 0.95
